Map basis: Natural Earth country boundaries (10 m), drawn only inside the frame: the eleven South-East Asian states are shaded. India, Bangladesh, China and Taiwan appear as plain outlines only; nothing is plotted in India, and its towns, airports, ports and rivers are left out. Disputed areas are described, not settled: the South China Sea (overlapping claims by China, Taiwan, Vietnam, the Philippines, Malaysia and Brunei; China rejects the 2016 arbitral award), the Spratly and Paracel features, Scarborough Shoal, and the Thai-Cambodian border. Most positions are approximate; each tooltip gives its source and whether it is verified in this build. Events reflect reports up to 10 October 2026.

Darkweb & Threat Intel…
Sources: Ransomware.live (leak-site claims - group claims, not verified facts); Telegram threat-intel channels; and dark-web RSS sources reached through Tor that you verify yourself (data/onion_sources.json).
To read onion sources you need Tor on this PC: open Tor Browser (it listens on port 9150) or install and start the Tor service (port 9050). Keep it running, then press Refresh. Nothing is needed for the clear-web and Telegram sources. This dashboard never opens .onion pages in a browser and does not search or crawl the dark web.
